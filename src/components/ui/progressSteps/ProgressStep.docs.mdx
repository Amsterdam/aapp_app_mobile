Use Progress Step to show where someone is in a sequence and what still comes next.

<Primary />

## When to use

Use Progress Step in short flows, timelines, or checklists where people benefit from seeing both their current position and the remaining sequence.

## Key states to review

- active versus planned versus completed progress
- numbered steps compared with completed steps that switch to a confirmation icon
- the last step, where the connector should stop cleanly
- expanded timeline content, where the connector should continue through the revealed content

## Do's and don'ts

### Do's

- Keep step labels clear enough to scan quickly
- Match the visual status to the real state of the journey
- Check that long content still preserves the sense of sequence

### Don'ts

- Use progress steps for unrelated items that do not form a sequence
- Mark a step as complete before the user or system has actually finished it
- Add extra detail inside a step if it makes the sequence harder to follow
