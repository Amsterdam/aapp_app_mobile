## Key states to review

- Permit holder and visitor accounts, including a visitor who has selected a
  license plate.
- An inactive permit, which explains when it becomes available and hides
  session-start actions.
- Permits with no end time and accounts with zero, two, or six upcoming
  sessions.

## Do and don't

- Do distinguish an inactive permit from an active permit with no sessions.
- Do show the selected license plate when visitor sessions are available.
- Don't imply that a visitor's sessions are visible before a license plate is
  selected.
